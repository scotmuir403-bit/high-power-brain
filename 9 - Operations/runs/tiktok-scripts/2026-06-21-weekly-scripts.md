The problem isn't the AI. It's the way you're talking to it.

Here's the structure: Role, Task, Context, Format.

Role — tell the AI who it is. "You are a content writer for a small Scottish jewellery business."
Task — tell it what to do. "Write a 200-word email newsletter for my customers."
Context — give it the details. "This week's offer is 20% off earrings. Warm tone, not salesy."
Format — tell it what you want back. "Three short paragraphs. No jargon."

Four parts. That's it.

Takes thirty extra seconds to set up. Saves you thirty minutes of editing on the other end.

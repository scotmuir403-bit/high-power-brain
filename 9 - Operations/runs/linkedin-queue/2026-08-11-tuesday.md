Most small business owners use AI wrong for content.

Not wrong as in badly. Wrong as in backwards.

They open ChatGPT, type "write me a LinkedIn post about my business," and get something generic that sounds like it was written by a committee.

Then they either post it and feel vaguely embarrassed, or delete it and decide AI doesn't work.

Here's the problem: the AI doesn't know your business, your audience, or how you sound. You gave it nothing to work with. Generic in, generic out.

The fix is simpler than most people think.

One prompt structure. Three inputs:

1. Who your audience is (be specific — not "small business owners," but "small business owners who are drowning in admin and don't know where to start with AI")

2. What they care about most (not what you want to say — what they actually need to hear)

3. What you want to say this week (one idea, not five)

That's it.

Those three inputs, consistently, will give you content that sounds like you — because it's built on what you actually know about your customers.

I use this same structure to build content systems for small business clients. The AI generates. They review and post. A full week of content in under twenty minutes.

Not because the AI is magic. Because the input is specific.

The tool is only as good as what you give it.

If you're struggling with AI for content — this is usually why.

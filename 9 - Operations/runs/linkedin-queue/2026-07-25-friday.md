BCG published something this week worth knowing if you're in a physical job or a trade.

The headline: AI will reshape more jobs than it replaces.

Here's what that actually means in plain terms.

The parts of most jobs that are repetitive — the admin, the emails, the paperwork, the scheduling — those are what's being automated.

The skill. The experience. The judgement you've built over years or decades. That stays yours. There's no automation for a 30-year career in highway maintenance.

I've had this conversation with a lot of people in physical jobs. The fear is real. But in almost every case, it's aimed at the wrong thing.

The people coming out ahead aren't the ones avoiding AI. They're the ones figuring out which part of their work it can support — and using that to spend more time on the work that actually matters.

That shift is available to everyone. Not just tech people. Not just young people.

I started this at 48. Built around night shifts. First client now in progress.

If you're carrying the "AI will make me irrelevant" fear quietly — you're not alone. And the research says it's probably not pointing at what you think it's pointing at.

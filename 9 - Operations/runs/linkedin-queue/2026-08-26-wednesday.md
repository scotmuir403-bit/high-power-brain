I want to show you what a Done-With-You AI content system actually looks like for a small business.

Not the version from a webinar. The real version, as I'm building it right now.

The client gives me three things each week:

— Their key messages (what they want people to know)
— Any news or updates from the business that week
— A quick yes/no on whether anything has changed in their tone or priorities

I take those inputs. I feed them into a structured prompt in Claude. The AI outputs a week of social content — captions, angles, post ideas — in the client's voice.

I review it. Edit where needed. Send for approval.

Client's time commitment: about 10 minutes a week.

The cost of the AI tools running the system: under £20 a month.

The time it saves them: 5–6 hours a week they were previously spending either writing content themselves or not posting at all.

That's the real value. Not magic. Not complicated. Just a clear system with clear inputs and clear outputs.

The reason this works is the setup. Getting the tone of voice document right. Getting the prompt structure right. Knowing what to edit and what to leave.

That's the part that takes skill.

The AI does the drafting. A human does the thinking.

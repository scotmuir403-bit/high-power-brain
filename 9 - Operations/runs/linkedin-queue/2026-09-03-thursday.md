Something shifted in AI this month that I think is worth paying attention to if you run a small business.

Claude — one of the main AI tools I use — just launched a desktop agent called Cowork. About twenty pounds a month.

The difference from what most people think of as AI: instead of answering your questions and waiting for you to act on the answer, it can now take actions on your behalf. Browse the web. Open files. Handle tasks while you're doing something else.

In practical terms for a small business owner, that means instead of asking AI to draft a follow-up email and then copying it yourself — the agent can find the lead, draft the email, and manage the sending. While you're having lunch.

I want to be straight about this: I'm still testing it. I'd always review what any agent does before it goes out under my name.

But the direction of travel here is clear. AI is moving from "tool you use" to "assistant that acts." And the small businesses that understand that shift early are going to have a meaningful advantage.

Worth knowing about. Worth trying if you're curious.

Link to it is easy to find — just search "Claude Cowork." Twenty pounds a month to start exploring what autonomous AI can actually do for your business.

The question I get most from small business owners when I talk about AI content systems:

"What does it actually do?"

Fair question. Here's the plain English version.

Most small businesses are in the same position. They know content matters. They know they should be posting consistently. But sitting down to write something every day — or even every week — is the first thing that gets dropped when things get busy.

An AI content system handles the architecture of that problem.

Here's the number that's been sitting with me this week.

AI automation is now growing eight times faster than human activity online.

That means AI agents — software doing tasks automatically — are outpacing human internet activity by a factor of eight.

Most people read that and feel something between anxiety and overwhelm.
Here's my take.

The question isn't whether AI is changing things. Clearly it is.
The question is: are the tools working for you or just for everyone else?

A business owner who's set up a few simple automations — content drafting, email handling, meeting notes — has AI working on their behalf while they do the actual work.
A business owner doing all of it manually is watching it happen from the outside.

That gap is closing fast.

I'm not saying this to be dramatic.
I'm saying it because I've been in the "watching from the outside" camp.
And I know how it feels when you realise you've been waiting when you could have been starting.

The starting is easier than it looks.
That's what I try to show on here.

The most common reason small business owners don't post consistently isn't that they don't have anything to say.

It's that they hate writing.

They know their business inside out. They can talk about it for an hour without pausing. But put a blank screen in front of them and ask for a LinkedIn post — and nothing.

Here's a workflow that fixes this.

Step one: open a voice memo app on your phone. Speak for two minutes about something that happened in your business this week. A win. A conversation with a client. Something you noticed. Doesn't need to be polished.

Step two: get the transcript. Otter.ai does this free, or just use your phone's built-in transcription.

Step three: paste the transcript into Claude with one instruction — "Turn this into a LinkedIn post in a plain, professional tone. Sound like a real person."

That's it.

You'll spend maybe five minutes editing the output. The post will sound like you because it started as you talking.

The content was always there. The problem was the blank screen. This removes the blank screen.

If you run a small business and you know you should be posting but you keep running out of time and energy to write — this is worth trying once. It either works for you or it doesn't.

Most people who try it, use it every week from then on.

This is what week 2 of building a real client's AI content system looks like.

My first client is Scott Brodie — he runs SafeSite Companion, a safety tech app. He was spending hours every week writing content manually. Social posts, emails, updates. All from scratch.

Week 1: we mapped the brand voice. Went well.

Week 2: we hit the first wall.

The AI outputs were too generic. They sounded like AI, not like SafeSite. Not like Scott.

So we went back.

We tightened the prompts. Added more of their actual language. Gave the system more examples of how they talk about their work.

Three hours of work. And then it clicked.

The content started sounding like them.

Here's the honest bit about building AI systems for clients:

It's not a straight line. There's always a wall somewhere in the first two weeks. The job is to find it quickly and fix it — not panic when it shows up.

Most people selling AI systems make this look effortless.

OpenAI said this week they expect to have AGI — artificial general intelligence — by the end of 2026.

That headline is doing a lot of damage to a lot of people's motivation.

Here's what I actually think about it, as someone building an AI content business from a kitchen table in Scotland.

There's a massive gap between what a research lab announces and what shows up in your business tomorrow morning. That gap is measured in years. The history of tech is full of announcements that took a decade to reach everyday life.

The AI available to you today — right now, for free or close to it — is already capable of saving small business owners significant time. Writing content. Handling customer emails. Automating repetitive admin. Building simple workflows that run without you.

Most people aren't using it. Not because it's hard. Because they haven't started.

The people using AGI as a reason to wait are skipping over the practical tools that already exist and already work.

I'm not dismissing the significance of where AI is heading. But I'm a lot more interested in what it can do for a small business owner this week than in what it might be able to do in 2028.

Start with what's in front of you. That's the whole advice.

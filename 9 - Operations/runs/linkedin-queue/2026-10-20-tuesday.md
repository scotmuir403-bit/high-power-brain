Most small business owners use AI like a fancy Google search.

Type a question. Get an answer. Close the tab. Wonder why it doesn't save them any time.

Here's the shift that actually changes things.

AI isn't a search engine. It's more like a thinking partner who's read everything and never gets tired.

The people saving real time with Claude aren't asking it questions. They're giving it context.

They say: here's what I'm trying to do, here's what I've tried, here's what I'm stuck on. Help me think.

I use it to plan my week. Draft client communications. Figure out what to do next when I'm not sure.

The rule I've landed on: Claude is as useful as the information you give it.

Garbage in → slightly better garbage out.
Context in → genuinely useful output.

One experiment worth running this week: instead of asking Claude a question, tell it your actual situation. The full picture. What you're trying to achieve and where you're stuck.

See what comes back.

It takes five minutes. The difference in output is significant.

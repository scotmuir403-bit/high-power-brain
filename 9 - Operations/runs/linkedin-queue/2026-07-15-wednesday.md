I've been explaining what I do to people lately and I want to get better at it.

So here's the plain English version.

I build AI content systems for small businesses.

In practice, that means:

I spend time understanding your business — who you're talking to, what you'd never say, how you actually sound when a conversation is going well. That becomes a brief document. One document that tells the AI everything it needs to write in your voice instead of a generic one.

I build the prompt structure around it. The specific way we feed your brief back into every content request so the output stays consistent every week.

I set up the review step. Because AI never goes straight to publish — a human eye on it first is part of the system, not an afterthought. Takes five minutes. Usually one or two small edits.

End result: a week of content without the business owner having to create it from scratch.

They approve it. They post it. They don't have to write it.

Setup takes a few days. Once it's running, it runs.

I'm building this for my first client right now. Real system. Real business. Real feedback going back and forth.

If that sounds like something your business needs and you want to talk through whether it would work for you — DM me. Happy to have an honest conversation about it.

Most "side hustle" content you see online looks like this:

Laptop on a beach. Coffee in hand. Sunrise behind them.

Here's what mine actually looks like.

Monday night: in a van on the motorway at 2am. Road signs. Cones. Headlights.

Tuesday afternoon: kitchen table. Laptop. Four hours sleep behind me.

That's the window. That's where this business gets built.

Two hours, maybe two and a half, before the fog sets in properly.

I use those two hours for the AI content work. Review scripts, refine the client system, post content, plan the next day.

Then it's dinner, a bit of time with my partner, and back to bed before another shift.

This isn't a success story yet. It's a work in progress.

But I've got a client conversation in progress. Content going out every day. A system that runs without me touching it constantly.

And all of that was built in Tuesday afternoon fog sessions and the odd Sunday evening.

If you're trying to build something around a demanding job and a real life — I get it.

You don't need a perfect setup. You just need a consistent window and something worth doing.

That's the honest version of this.

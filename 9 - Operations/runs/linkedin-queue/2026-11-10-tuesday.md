The most common mistake I see small business owners make with AI:

They try to learn everything before they use anything.

You don't need to understand how AI works to use it. You need to know what problem you want to solve first.

Here's the exercise I walk clients through before we touch any tool:

Write down three things you do every week that take longer than they should. Admin tasks. Repetitive writing. Things you do from memory on autopilot.

Circle the one that wastes the most of your time.

That's your starting point. Not a course, not a YouTube playlist — that specific task.

Once you've got the task, you've got the brief. You take that brief to Claude or ChatGPT, describe the task clearly, and ask it to help you build a template or workflow for it.

That's the whole first step. One task. One session. One hour.

Most people never get there because they're waiting to feel ready. The feeling of being ready doesn't come before you start. It comes after.

Week 2 building an AI content system for a real client.

Last week was the setup: content brief, brand voice guide, weekly prompt workflow.

This week should have been the handover.

It wasn't quite that simple.

The outputs from week 1 were close. But "close" isn't good enough for someone to put their brand name on.

So we went back to the tone guide. Read the outputs out loud. Small adjustments. Tested again.

Two hours on one document — getting the client's voice right so an AI can approximate it consistently.

That's the bit they skip in the "how to get AI content clients" videos.

Setting up the system is maybe 20% of the work. Calibrating it to a specific person's voice — that's where the actual service is.

Week 2 done. Content is starting to sound like him.

Still building this in public. Around night shifts. First client in progress.

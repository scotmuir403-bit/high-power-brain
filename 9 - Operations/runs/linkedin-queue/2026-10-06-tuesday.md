If you're a small business owner spending more than two hours a week on content — you're doing it the hard way.

Here's what most people I talk to are missing.

It's not that AI tools don't work for content.
It's that they're being used like a fancy search engine.
Generic question in → generic output out.

The difference is the prompt.

When you give AI the right context — your voice, your audience, your specific idea, the tone you want — what comes back is a first draft you need fifteen minutes to review. Not an hour to rebuild from scratch.

Three things to include in every content prompt:

1. Who you are (industry, tone, how you talk)
2. Who you're talking to (specific, not general)
3. What you want it to do (write / rewrite / simplify / expand)

That's it.
Two minutes of setup saves two hours of editing.

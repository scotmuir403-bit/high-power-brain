A lot of small business owners tell me the same thing:

"I know I should be posting more. I just don't have the time to sit down and write."

That's exactly the problem I'm building a solution for.

Here's what a Done-With-You AI content system actually looks like in practice. Not the theory — what it is day to day.

**Part 1: The template library.**
We build a set of post templates based on your business, your customers' questions, and what you actually want to be known for. These live in a simple document. They're yours permanently.

**Part 2: The weekly brief.**
Every week, one short form. Takes about 5 minutes. What happened this week? Any promotions? Anything you want to highlight? That's your input.

**Part 3: AI drafts, you review.**
The AI uses your templates and your brief to produce a week of content. You read it. You approve it (or tweak it). It goes out.

That's the whole system.

You're not writing posts from scratch. You're not briefing an agency. You're not paying someone £1,000/month. You're reviewing content once a week and pressing approve.

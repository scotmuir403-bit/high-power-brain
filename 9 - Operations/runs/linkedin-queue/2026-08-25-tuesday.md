The number one reason small business owners don't use AI tools isn't what people think.

It's not technical ability. It's not cost.

It's the prompt.

Most people open ChatGPT or Claude, type something vague, get something generic back, and conclude that AI isn't useful.

The tool isn't the problem. The input is.

Here's the simplest fix I know.

Every time you ask an AI tool to write something for you — give it three things:

1. Who you are and what you do (one sentence)
2. Who you're talking to (one sentence)
3. What you want it to sound like (e.g. "direct, plain, no jargon — like a person, not a brand")

That third one is the bit most people skip. And it's the reason the output sounds like a press release instead of you.

When you add "sounds like me" to the prompt — the output changes completely.

I use a version of this structure for every piece of content I build for clients. One template. Adapted per situation. The AI does the drafting. I do the editing.

The whole process takes about 20 minutes once you've got the system set up.

If you're a small business owner spending hours every week on content — this is the first thing I'd change.

Not the tool. The prompt.

Try it this week. Open Claude or ChatGPT. Give it those three things. See what comes back.

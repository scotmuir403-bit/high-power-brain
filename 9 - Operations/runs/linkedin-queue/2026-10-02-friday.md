Honest update. End of week.

First client — Scott Brodie, founder of SafeSite Companion — is still in progress.

That means: the conversation is alive, the scope is getting clearer, and I'm actively building the system.

What it doesn't mean: signed contract, money in the bank, success story to screenshot.

I'm posting this because too much of what you see online about starting a service business goes from "first client call" to "closed deal" with nothing in between.

The in-between is:
— Refining what you're actually offering based on what they actually need
— Figuring out the tech stack that fits their situation
— Building parts of the system before you've been paid, to prove it's real

It's also the most valuable part.

Every conversation and every build decision is teaching me what this service actually is — not what I thought it was when I started.

That's building in public. The process, not just the outcome.

Next week I'll have a clearer picture of where this is heading.
I'll post that too.

If you're doing the same — building something in the messy middle where nothing's confirmed yet — I see you.

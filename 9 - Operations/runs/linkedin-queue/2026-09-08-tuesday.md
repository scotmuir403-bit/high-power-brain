GPT-6 dropped last week. A lot of people I know saw that news and felt further behind than ever.

I get that. Every time a new model lands, the gap feels wider.

Here's what I've learned after 18 months of doing this around night shifts.

The tools don't wait for you to feel ready. They just keep coming.

Which means waiting until you "understand AI better" is not a strategy. It's just a way of staying comfortable while time moves.

I started in February last year with a free Claude account, zero automation experience, and ten hours a week between shifts. I didn't understand how any of it worked. I just started using it for one real thing — drafting content.

That one thing became a process. The process became a system. The system became my first client.

Here's the practical bit.

You don't need GPT-6. You don't need the latest model. You need one tool and one task you already do manually that AI could do faster.

Pick the task. Open the tool. Spend 20 minutes. That's it.

The distance between where you are and where you want to be is smaller than the news cycle makes it feel.

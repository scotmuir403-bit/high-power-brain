I'm going to tell you exactly why I'm building this.

Not the polished version. The actual one.

I'm 49. I've worked highway maintenance and signage for 30 years. Night shifts. Early starts. The kind of work that takes something out of you physically, week after week.

I'm not miserable. I'm grateful for the work. But I started asking myself a question about three years ago that I couldn't shake:

**What happens if my body says no before I'm ready?**

Thirty years on the road, on your feet, in all weathers — there's a ceiling on how long that goes. And I wasn't doing anything about it.

So I started learning about AI. Not because I'm technical. Not because I had money to invest. Because I needed something that didn't have a physical ceiling.

Took me a while to find the angle. Tried a few things that didn't fit. Then I landed on this:

AI content systems for small businesses. Set up once. Posts written every week. No ongoing tech headaches.

I'm not ahead of you. I'm figuring this out with one client in progress and another lead warming up. Still on night shifts. Still building this on the side.

But I'm building it. Week by week. That's the whole point.

If you're a small business owner who wants consistent content without spending hours on it every week — that's exactly what I'm working on.

Free starter kit in the bio. That's where I started.

Two years ago I'd never heard of Claude. Didn't know what an AI content system was. Didn't know what n8n was.

I knew how to fix road signs in the dark.

30 years in highway maintenance. Still working nights.

The turning point wasn't dramatic. It was a Sunday afternoon. I watched someone — probably my age — explain how they'd set up an automated content system for their business. In one afternoon.

I thought: that's learnable.

So I started. Kitchen table. 2am after shifts. I didn't understand most of it at first. I just kept going.

Here's where it stands now:

— First client deal in progress. A safety app for construction sites.
— Posting every week about what I'm building and what I'm learning.
— Still on nights. That bit hasn't changed yet.

I'm not sharing this to impress anyone. I'm sharing it because nobody showed me the middle part when I started. The "it's in progress and I don't know exactly how it ends" part.

If you're 40-something, working a physical job, and wondering whether it's too late to build something on the side with AI — it isn't.

You just have to start before you feel ready.

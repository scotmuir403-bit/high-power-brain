The headline last month said AI was behind nearly 40% of job cuts.

Here's my honest take, for what it's worth.

The fear is real. I'm not going to pretend it isn't. If you work in admin, data processing, or a role that's mostly repetitive tasks — that fear is pointing at something real.

But here's what I keep noticing when I talk to people in everyday jobs.

The ones feeling most threatened are the ones who haven't started using AI yet.

The ones who've been using it — even at a basic level — don't talk about replacement. They talk about what they can do now that they couldn't do before.

That's not a coincidence.

The thing AI is replacing is tasks. Not people who learn to work alongside it.

I'm 49. Night shift highway maintenance. I started learning this stuff last year with no tech background and no idea what I was doing.

I'm not an expert. I'm not a guru. I'm just someone who decided not to wait until the picture got clearer.

If you're sitting with that fear right now — that's understandable. It's also a reason to start, not a reason to wait.

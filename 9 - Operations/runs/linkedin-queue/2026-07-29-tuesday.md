72% of small businesses now use AI tools every week.

Most of them are still spending hours on the tasks AI should be handling.

Here's the gap.

Most people use AI the same way they used Google 15 years ago. Ask a question. Get an answer. Move on.

Useful. But not saving you hours.

The shift that actually changes how much time you get back isn't about a better tool. It's about moving from a tool you use occasionally to a system that runs in the background.

That means knowing which tasks to hand over. Building a simple workflow. Running the workflow instead of starting from scratch each time.

For most small businesses, that's: social media content, customer follow-up emails, document summaries, and weekly reporting.

Not complicated. Just consistent.

The difference between the businesses I talk to who say AI "kind of helps" versus the ones who say AI "saves me 3 hours a week" — is a system. Not a smarter tool.

If you're in the first group and want to move to the second — free resource in the bio is where I'd start.

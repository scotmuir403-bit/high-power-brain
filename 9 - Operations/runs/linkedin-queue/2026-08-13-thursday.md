82% of small businesses are now using AI tools.

That number is from the SBE Council's 2026 survey. And the businesses reporting the most benefit? They're saving £500-2,000 a month and recovering 20+ hours.

Here's the part that interests me more than the headline number.

The 18% not using AI yet.

Why not?

From the conversations I've had — it's not cost (tools are cheaper than ever). It's not technical skill (the tools don't require it anymore).

It's the same thing it's always been:

"I don't know where to start."

That's the gap. And it's entirely fixable.

The businesses getting the most out of AI aren't the ones that bought the most expensive tools. They're the ones that picked one problem — usually content, admin, or customer communication — and built a simple system around it.

One problem. One tool. One workflow.

That's the whole entry point.

I've watched business owners spend months trying to figure out their "AI strategy" before using a single tool. And I've watched others set up a working content automation in an afternoon and wonder why they waited so long.

The people in the second group didn't know more. They just started with something specific instead of trying to understand everything first.

If you're in the 18%: pick one task you do every week that eats time. Start there.

That's it. That's the whole thing.

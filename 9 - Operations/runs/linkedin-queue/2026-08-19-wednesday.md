I've been building an AI content system for a small business client — a real app developer, SafeSite Companion — for a few months now.

I want to give you the honest version of what that looks like.

Because it's not what I expected.

The conversation is still in progress. No signed deal. I'm saying that plainly because I built this whole thing on honesty and pretending otherwise would be the exact kind of thing I'm trying to stay clear of.

But here's what I've learned from that one conversation that no YouTube video or course taught me.

Real clients ask the questions you haven't thought to ask yourself.

They want to know: what exactly does the system produce each week? How much of it is AI and how much is my own voice? What happens if I don't like the output? What does it cost to run on my side?

Those are completely fair questions. And having to answer them out loud has forced me to make the system better.

The part that's surprised me most: the wait between conversations doesn't mean the deal is dead. It means they're doing their due diligence. That's a good sign. That's a serious buyer.

What I've learned about client work I'd never have learned just building this for myself:

You have to show the system before someone buys into it. Talking about what AI can do is interesting. Showing what it produces for a business like theirs is different.

If you're at the stage of wondering what building something real looks like — it looks like this. Slow, iterative, honest conversations.

Worth it. Nowhere near done. Still going.

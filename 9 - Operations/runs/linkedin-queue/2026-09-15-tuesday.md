The biggest shift in AI right now isn't a new tool. It's a new behaviour.

AI agents.

Most people still think of AI like a search engine you can have a conversation with. You ask, it answers. You type, it types back.

AI agents are different. They don't just answer — they act. Multiple steps, in a row, without you doing each one.

A real example: tell an AI agent to find 10 local businesses in your area without active Instagram accounts, pull their contact details, write a personalised email to each one, and log who replies. It does all of that. On its own.

For small business owners, this changes something important.

The most expensive thing in running a small business isn't staff costs or marketing. It's your time doing things that could be done for you. The chasing. The copying across. The admin that fills the gaps between the actual work.

AI agents are starting to handle that layer.

You don't need to know how they work. You need to know they exist and start asking what tasks in your week could be handed off.

Most businesses are still treating AI like a fancy calculator. The ones who treat it like an assistant are already moving faster.

Where are you on that spectrum?

I started building this in the gaps.

Not "I left my job to pursue my passion."
Not "I took a leap of faith."

I kept showing up to night shifts.
And I kept sitting at the kitchen table at 2am learning AI tools I'd never used before.

For months, I didn't know if any of it would work.
I was 48. Career in highway maintenance. No background in tech. No online business experience.

Just a clear reason to start — and no better plan.

Eleven months later:

First client deal in progress.
A content system built entirely on AI tools.
A TikTok channel reaching people I'd never have met from behind a fence on the M8.

Still on nights.
Still figuring parts of this out.
But building something that's mine.

I post this not to inspire — but because if you're somewhere in the middle of your own "I don't know if this is going to work" phase, I want you to see the real version of someone else in it.

The quiet phase is the one most people don't get through.

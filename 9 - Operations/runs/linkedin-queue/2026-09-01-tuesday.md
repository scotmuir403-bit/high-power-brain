If you're writing social posts one at a time, twenty minutes each, every day — there's a better way.

Here's exactly how I write a week of LinkedIn content in under 20 minutes.

Open Claude (or ChatGPT — either works). Use this prompt:

"I run a [type of business]. Write me 5 LinkedIn posts for this week. Each should sound honest and plain — no corporate language. My audience is [who they are]. One post per day, each focused on a different angle: a practical tip, a question for the audience, a win or progress update, an honest mistake or lesson, and a behind-the-scenes moment."

Hit send.

You'll get five posts in about thirty seconds.

Then — and this is the bit most people skip — edit them. Put your voice in. Add the specific detail only you know. Cut any word that sounds like it came from a press release.

The AI builds the structure. You build the voice.

That one shift took me months to figure out. Once you get it — content goes from a daily drain to a weekly task.

If you try this, let me know what you find. The prompts always need a bit of tweaking for your specific business — happy to look at anyone's if they want a second pair of eyes.

There's a lot of noise in the AI space right now about "agents."

If you've seen it and wondered what it means for your business — here's the plain English version.

Until recently, AI was reactive. You typed something, it responded. That's it.

An AI agent is different. It's AI that can take a goal and work toward it on its own — checking things, opening tools, taking steps — without you sitting there managing every move.

Right now this is mostly being used in technical settings. Coding, research, complex workflows.

But the direction is clear. Within the next couple of years, these agents will be handling the kind of repetitive admin that eats up a small business owner's afternoon.

Chasing invoices. Responding to routine enquiries. Processing and sorting information.

Here's what this means for you as a small business owner:

It's not a reason to panic. It's a reason to start building familiarity with AI now — so that when these tools land in the everyday software you already use, you're not starting from zero.

The business owners I talk to who are already using basic AI tools consistently are six months ahead of the ones who are still waiting to feel ready.

You don't need to understand agents today. You need to start using the basics today.

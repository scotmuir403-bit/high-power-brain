Six months ago I pivoted this whole thing.

I'd been trying to build an audience promoting digital marketing courses.

It wasn't working. Felt off. Felt like I was wearing someone else's clothes.

So I stopped. Decided to just do the thing I was actually doing.

Building AI content systems for small businesses. Setting them up once so the content writes itself every week.

That's it. Nothing fancy.

In the six months since:
— Started posting consistently (every day for months now)
— Built a system that runs my own content at 14 posts a week
— Had my first real client conversation
— Got a starter kit written and in the bio

Still on night shifts. Still at the kitchen table. Still figuring parts of it out as I go.

The numbers aren't big yet. I'm not going to pretend otherwise. But they're going in the right direction — and more importantly, this feels real.

I'm building the thing I actually use, for people who actually need it, and telling the truth about where I am.

That's what I've got.

If you're following this journey — thanks for being here.

Had my first proper client call this week.

Real conversation. Someone interested in the AI content system I build for small businesses. Not a vague chat — a proper "let's understand what this looks like for my business" conversation.

Here's the honest version of how I prepared.

I used AI to anticipate the questions before they came up. What would someone in their position want to know? What might make them hesitant? What's the simplest way to explain what the system actually does?

I wrote that all out. Read it back. Trimmed it. Then I had a conversation — not a presentation.

I didn't have a deck. Didn't have a formal proposal. Just a clear explanation of what I build, who it's for, and a simple next step if it made sense.

I don't know yet how it lands. That's still playing out.

But what I do know: AI helped me prepare better than I would have on my own. And building in public — sharing this as it happens — is keeping me accountable in a way that the private version of this wouldn't.

That's the week. First client call. More to come.

If you're building something alongside a day job and want to compare notes — I'm always up for that conversation.

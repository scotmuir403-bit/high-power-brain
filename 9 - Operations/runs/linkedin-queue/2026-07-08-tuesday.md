Most small businesses are using AI wrong.

Not because they're bad at it. Because nobody's shown them how.

Here's the mistake I see constantly:

They ask ChatGPT a question. Get a generic answer. Think "that's not quite right." Tweak it. Accept something mediocre. Repeat.

The fix is simple — and it's called a system prompt.

Instead of starting every conversation from scratch, you give the AI context about your business up front.

Who you are. Who your customers are. What you sell. What your tone sounds like. What you never say.

You write this once. Takes about 45 minutes. Then you paste it at the start of every session.

The difference in output quality is significant. It's the difference between asking a stranger to write something for your business, and asking someone who already knows your business inside out.

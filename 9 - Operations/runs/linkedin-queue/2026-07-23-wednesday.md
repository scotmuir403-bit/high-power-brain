I work highway maintenance. Night shifts. Have done for 30 years.

The question I get asked most often: when do you find the time to build this?

Honest answer: the 90 minutes before I leave for a shift.

Before I started taking it seriously, I used that window to scroll, eat, and think about all the things I wasn't getting done.

About eight months ago I blocked it in my calendar and labelled it "build time."

The rule: AI tools only. No admin. No planning about planning. Just build.

Week one — one piece of content.
Week two — one part of a client system.
Week three — first client conversation.

That's three weeks of 90-minute sessions. Not three weeks of full-time work.

The lesson I didn't expect: you don't need a bigger window. You need to stop treating the small one like it's too small to matter.

I'm 49. First client in progress. Still on the night shift.

Not because it's easy. Because it's possible.

For anyone building something in the gaps right now — I see you.

There's something the AI headlines keep getting wrong about physical jobs.

The narrative is: AI is coming for everyone. White collar, blue collar, all of it. Run.

The data says something more specific.

The jobs AI is replacing hardest right now are white-collar and entry-level. Admin, data processing, basic analysis, entry-level content work — that's where the genuine disruption is happening.

The jobs that need physical dexterity, on-site judgement, and 20 years of tacit knowledge? Current AI genuinely can't do those. Not even close.

That doesn't mean physical jobs are safe forever. It means the timeline is different.

What it also means: if you're in a trade or physical work, the AI opportunity isn't about survival. It's about getting ahead.

The stuff around your job — the admin, the reporting, the scheduling, the content, the customer communication — that's automatable now. Today. With free tools.

The people who figure that out in the next 12 months will look dramatically more productive than their peers in 24 months.

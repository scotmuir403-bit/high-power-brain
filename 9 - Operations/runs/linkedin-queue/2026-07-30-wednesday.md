OpenAI launched GPT-Live this month.

Full-duplex voice AI — it can listen and speak at the same time, like an actual conversation instead of turn-by-turn audio exchanges.

Here's what that means for small businesses, in plain terms.

Right now, most customer interactions happen in one of three ways: text, phone calls, or forms.

AI has been creeping into the text layer for years. GPT-Live starts reaching into the phone call layer.

Near future: an AI that answers a customer's call, handles common questions, books appointments, takes details, escalates to a human only when it needs to.

Not a replacement for human relationships in business. A first filter.

Most small businesses can't afford a receptionist or a customer service hire. This kind of technology starts to change that equation.

I'm still watching how this develops. But the direction is clear.

Voice is becoming the next interface for AI — and the businesses that understand what's coming will be a step ahead of the ones still surprised by it.

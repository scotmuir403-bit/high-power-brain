Most people use AI like a Google search.

Type in a question. Get an answer. Close the tab.

That's not wrong — but it's missing the thing that makes AI actually save you time.

Context.

Here's the prompt structure I use every week to write my content:

"I run a [type of business] for [type of customer]. My voice is [plain description]. Here's what happened in my business this week: [one specific thing]. Write me three short social posts in that voice."

That's it.

Takes 5 minutes to write once. AI does the first draft. You read it, tweak it, post it.

I've used this every week for six months. My first client uses it now.

Two to three hours of content time, down to twenty minutes.

The part people miss: you still need to review it. AI does the draft — you make it yours. The human is still in the loop.

But the hours spent staring at a blank page wondering where to start? That bit goes away.

Try it this week and let me know how it lands in the comments.

There's a conversation going round about AI and jobs.

Most of it is noise. But some of it matters.

BCG published research recently saying AI will reshape more jobs than it replaces.

Reshape is the word I keep coming back to.

Because in my experience that's exactly what's happening. Not replacement — redistribution of effort.

The admin work that used to take three hours now takes 30 minutes. The writing that used to require a specialist can now be done by the business owner with the right tools. The content that used to need an agency can now be set up as a system.

What's being replaced is the repetitive, low-skill part of roles.

What's getting more valuable is the judgment, the relationships, the trust.

If you're a small business owner, that's mostly good news. You've got the judgment. You've got the relationships. You've got the trust.

What you might not have is the tools.

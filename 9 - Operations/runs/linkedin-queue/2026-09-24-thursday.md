The mistake I see most often when small business owners try AI tools.

They ask it for the output without giving it the input.

"Write me a LinkedIn post."

About what? For who? In what tone? What do you want people to feel after reading it?

The AI doesn't know any of that. So it guesses. And generic content is what you get when AI guesses about you.

The fix isn't a better tool. It's a better brief.

Here's the minimum a brief needs to be useful:

— What your business does in one sentence
— Who you're talking to specifically (not "small business owners" — "a plumber who wants more steady contract work and less time on admin")
— What tone you want (professional but plain, conversational, direct)
— What you want people to do after reading it

Give Claude or ChatGPT those four things and ask it to write the post. Then compare that output to what you got without the brief.

The difference is significant. The editing time drops. The output sounds like it came from someone who actually knows what they're talking about.

Most people skip the brief because it feels like extra work. It's not. It's the work that makes everything else easier.

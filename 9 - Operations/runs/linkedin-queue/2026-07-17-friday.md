End of week thought.

This is what building an AI content business around night shifts actually looks like.

This week: one client session, 14 TikTok scripts planned, two meetings rescheduled because of shift patterns, and one moment on Wednesday where I nearly convinced myself I was too far behind to catch up.

I wasn't.

But that thought — the "I'm too far behind" thought — visits regularly. Usually around 2am heading into work, when I'm calculating how long this is going to take.

What I've learned to do with it: write it down. Name it clearly. Then ask: "Is this based on evidence or exhaustion?"

Usually it's exhaustion.

The evidence is: one client in progress, a content system that works, a growing audience that's genuinely the right audience. Slow progress. Real progress.

I'm not telling you this to be inspiring. I'm telling you because if you're building something on the side and that thought visits you too — you're not the only one.

The goal is still to replace the night shift income. That's still in front of me.

But it's less in front of me than it was six months ago.

That's enough for this week.
